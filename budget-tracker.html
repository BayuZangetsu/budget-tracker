➕ Add Pocket

Default /mo is a template: the Auto Allocate button uses it as its target every month. You can leave it blank and fill it in later under Manage Plot Template.

⚡ Allocate Funds to a Pocket

Quick way: enter your income, then click Auto Allocate. Every pocket is allocated to its template value at once, with no manual entry per pocket. The modal that appears can be adjusted for this month only — changing Target /mo there does not change the template.

Keep allocating by hand when you want to go above the template, for example Rp 500,000 into the Electricity pocket.

📋 Copy & Paste Data Between Months

Open this month → Copy Data → switch to another month → Paste Data. Pockets that do not exist yet are created automatically.

The copied data contains income, expenses, allocations and pocket transfers.

Data is saved automatically in your browser (localStorage). Switch month/year any time.
